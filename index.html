How to play

  1. Watch the highlighted squares carefully.
  2. When they disappear, tap the same squares.
  3. Find every square without making a mistake.
  4. Complete a level to unlock the next one.

Each grid has 20 levels. Larger grids and higher levels challenge your working memory.

Challenge settings

Memory display time decreases gradually as levels rise. Each new level adds more squares.
